# Privacy Policy — Khai Tracker

*Effective date: [insert]. Owner: Khai Foods Pvt Ltd, India.*

## 1. What this app is
Khai Tracker is the data-collection tool for the Khai Foods Phase-0 study (an
observational lifestyle pilot). It is a research tool, not a medical device,
and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, cure, or prevention
of any disease.

## 2. Data we collect
Only what a participant enters voluntarily: participant code (not real name/
phone), age group, health conditions, medications, food items, supplements,
self-ratings (energy/mood/sleep), sleep, optional fasting blood sugar, and free
notes. A locally generated device identifier is used as a sync key.

## 3. How it is used
- To support the participant's own tracking (data stays on the device).
- Anonymous, code-based aggregation for study analytics (adherence, trends).
- Reviewed only under the informed-consent terms the participant accepted.

## 4. What we do NOT do
No advertising, no marketing use, no sale or rental of data, no third-party
sharing, no tracking/analytics SDKs, no permission to contacts/photos/location.

## 5. Storage & security
- On-device storage (localStorage) and, if sync is enabled, a server database
  encrypted in transit (TLS). Access to the server is keyed by a coordinator
  token; the clinician view is limited to a participant link shared by the study
  coordinator.

## 6. Retention & deletion
Study data is retained for the study period and statutory audit norms, then
anonymised or deleted. You may delete your device data at any time via the
app's Reset control. To request server removal, email the coordinator (address
below) with your participant code; we will purge your rows within 7 days.

## 7. Children
The app is not directed at children under 13; pilot participants are adults.

## 8. Contact
Privacy questions or deletion requests: [coordinator email] (to be set once the
company mailbox exists). You may also contact via a message in the app's Settings
→ "About/Contact".

## 9. Changes
We will notify material changes in-app before continuing to sync; continued use
after notice constitutes acceptance. This policy is a commitment that the app's
current release honours; where store app bundles are distributed, this policy is
linkable from the store listing.
