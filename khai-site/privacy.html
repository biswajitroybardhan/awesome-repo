Plain version: the app collects only what you type or tap. It encrypts each day on your phone. There are no ads, no analytics, no account, and no sale of data. Sync, sharing and reminders are all off unless you turn them on.

1. What this app is

Khai Tracker is a measurement and record-keeping tool for participants in a Khai Foods observational pilot. It is not a medical device, gives no medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, cure or prevention of disease, and never changes a prescription. Decisions about care belong to a qualified clinician.

2. What you enter

Only what you choose to type or tap:

  • a participant code assigned by your coordinator — not your name, phone number or address;
  • age band and health conditions you select;
  • medicines you list, and whether you took, skipped or changed them each day;
  • food items and portions from the food library;
  • blood pressure, pulse, blood glucose (with fasting/post-food context), weight;
  • energy, mood, sleep and a free-text note.

The app does not read your contacts, photos, location, calendars, SMS, call logs, microphone or camera. It has no advertising identifier and no analytics SDK.

3. How it is stored

  • Each day's record is encrypted on the phone with AES-256-GCM. The key is generated inside the hardware-backed Android Keystore, is not exportable and is never sent anywhere.
  • The database file contains ciphertext only.
  • The app is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your record is not copied to Google Drive or to a new phone by the operating system.
  • An optional four-digit PIN gates the interface. It is stored only as a salted PBKDF2 hash and is not a cryptographic encryption key.

4. When data leaves the phone

Only in three situations, each initiated by you or your coordinator:

  1. Study sync (optional). If your coordinator gives you a server address, the app sends your records over HTTPS, keyed by your participant code and a random device ID. Without a server address, nothing is transmitted.
  2. Export or share. When you export, the app writes a file to its private cache and hands it to the app you pick in the system share sheet (mail, chat, Drive, printer). Khai never uploads it itself.
  3. Study operations. Your coordinator or clinician may review records on the study server under the consent you accepted. Access is limited to named study staff.

5. What we do not do

No advertising. No marketing. No sale or rental of data. No third-party analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDK. No profiling for decisions about your care. No collection of names, addresses, government identifiers or contact lists.

7. Retention and deletion

  • On the phone: data stays until you erase it. Settings → Privacy → Erase my data deletes records, the encryption key, profile, settings and PIN, irreversibly.
  • On the study server: retained for the study period and statutory audit requirements, then anonymised or deleted. To request earlier removal, email the contact with your participant code; rows are purged within seven days.
  • Uninstalling removes the local database and the Keystore key. Files you already exported are outside the app's control.

8. Children, international transfers and your choices

Children. The app is intended for adults (18+) in the pilot. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.

International transfers. If your study server is hosted outside your country, records are transferred over HTTPS under the study's data-processing agreement. The app does not choose a location; your coordinator does.

Your choices. You can decline consent at onboarding and not use the app; use it without sync; disable the reminder; erase everything at any time; and export a complete JSON copy before erasing.

Incidents and changes. If a confirmed breach affects your records, the study team will notify affected participants and the relevant authority as law requires. Material changes are presented in the app before further sync.

9. The browser demo is not the app

Demo warning. The browser demo is an illustrative sandbox clearly marked “demo”. It stores sample data in your browser's localStorage, with no encryption, no Android Keystore, no PIN, no sync, no reminders and no upload. Nothing leaves your browser. Do not enter real clinical records into it — use invented data only.
